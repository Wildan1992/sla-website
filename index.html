ABOUT SLA

Built for the region, run like a desk you can call.

SLA Service Company handles freight forwarding and transportation across sea, land and air from its office in Ainkawa, Erbil. Rather than routing you through a call centre, every shipment is followed by people you can reach directly — by phone or by email — from booking through to delivery.
