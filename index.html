QUESTIONS

Common questions, answered.

How long does customs clearance usually take?

It depends on the cargo type, documentation readiness, and destination — but delays are most often caused by missing or incorrect paperwork. Preparing documentation correctly before the shipment arrives is one of the biggest factors in keeping clearance fast.

Is there a minimum shipment size?

No — partial shipments (part-container or part-load) are available for smaller volumes, so you're not required to book a full container or full truck.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on mode (sea, land, air), route, cargo weight/volume, and timing. Send your shipment details and you'll receive a specific quote directly from the team — not a generic rate card.

Do you handle shipments outside Iraq?

Yes. SLA is based in Erbil, but coordinates freight to and from destinations worldwide, combining direct regional handling with partner networks for wider international reach.

Can you handle urgent or time-critical cargo?

Yes — express options are available across sea, land and air for shipments where timing matters more than cost.

How do I get in touch about a shipment already in progress?

Reach out directly by phone, WhatsApp, or email — the same point of contact follows your shipment from booking through delivery, so there's no need to explain your shipment from scratch to someone new.
